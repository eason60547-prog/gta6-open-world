Palmera Bay is an original 2D top-down open-world crime game built for make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty. Explore a sun-soaked seaside city on foot and by car, steal cars, build a wanted level and outrun the police, and run six story missions for the Bay Crew. Everything — city, characters, vehicles, story, art, music — is original; no Rockstar names, maps, or assets.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network requests: the game is plain static files under `game/`.

## Controls

WASD or arrow keys to move and drive · Shift to run · E to steal a car · Q to start a mission · Space / mouse to fight · P or Esc to pause. Progress saves automatically (localStorage).

## The game

- A 3600×2600 seaside city: roads, buildings, a central park, a market, a beach, and a harbor, with traffic and pedestrians.
- Wanted level 0–5 stars: police chase in cars and on foot, and the heat cools off if you lay low.
- Six story missions for Milo Reyes and the Bay Crew, from stealing a car to robbing Palmera Bank, with cash rewards.
- HUD with cash, health, wanted stars, speed, and ammo, plus a minimap with mission markers and police.
- Synthesized WebAudio sound: engine, sirens, gunfire, and mission music. No audio files.
